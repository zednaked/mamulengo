# The abduction: a night scene with the Mamulengo opening and closing

![A night countryside: the Mamulengo name and puppet open the scene, the view tilts down to hills, trees and a house; a pink flying saucer abducts a robot with a beam, runs off, and the name and puppet close the loop](abducao.webp)

[Watch in HD: MP4, 1280x720, 60 fps](abducao.mp4)

A 13.5-second loop built from two CC0 packs by Kenney (Alien UFO pack and Background
elements), the CC0 robot from `tests/boneco`, and the Mamulengo icon and name from
`exemplos/vinheta`, reused as instances.

| time | what happens |
|---|---|
| 0 to 2 s | night sky, a shooting star; the name arrives letter by letter and the puppet drops in on its strings |
| 2 to 3 s | the view tilts down to the countryside: every layer moves by its own depth (parallax) |
| 3 to 4 s | hills, fog, fireflies, trees swaying, a house with a lit window, the robot on the hill |
| 4 to 5.4 s | the saucer zigzags in and hovers over the robot, landing legs out; the robot throws its arms up |
| 5.4 to 8 s | the beam comes down and lights the ground and the trees, which bend towards it; stars rise and the robot spins up into the saucer, with a flash |
| 8 to 9 s | the beam retracts and the saucer runs off with a trail |
| 9 to 10 s | the view tilts back up to the sky |
| 10 to 13.5 s | the saucer crosses the sky and the letters land as it passes; the puppet drops in and bows; fade to black, which is where the loop starts |

| file | what it is |
|---|---|
| `disco.boneco` | the pink saucer, rigged from the Kenney SVG: body, pilot in the helmet (between the body and its front rim, so it sits inside the cockpit), landing legs, rim lights; animations `paira` (hover, legs out), `voa` and `foge` (legs tucked in, a trail on the run) |
| `arvore.boneco` | one tree per skin, pinned at the base; `balanca` and `vento` |
| `abducao.boneco` | the scene: the saucer, ten trees, the robot, nine letters and the puppet as instances |

**Depth.** Every layer sits on a bone that moves by its depth factor: stars 0.12, moon 0.15,
clouds 0.25, mountains 0.35, far hills 0.6, near hills and the saucer's plane 0.85, ground 1,
foreground silhouettes 1.3. The vertical tilt, a slow sideways camera drift and the camera
shakes (beam hit, flash, run) all go through the same factor, so the beam never leaves the robot.
The opening and the closing blur the whole countryside behind the name with an adjustment layer
(docs/NUCLEO.md "Ajuste").

**Light.** The page normals come from the art (`tools/relevo.ts`). The moon, the window, the beam
and the saucer's engine are point lights; the name and the puppet have a front light of their own,
in a nearer layer, lit only in the opening and the closing.

Generated by `npx tsx exemplos/abducao/gerar.ts`. The Kenney sources are in `fontes/` with their
licenses.

# A fake game made only with Mamulengo

![the game scene](jogo.gif)

`jogo.boneco` is a whole game screen in one file: a side-scrolling platformer with a parallax
background, floating platforms, spinning coins, a winged enemy, a jetpack power-up, a HUD and the
robot from [the showcase](../vitrine) as the hero. It loops in 8 s:

| time | what happens |
|---|---|
| 0.3 s | the robot jumps with its jets and picks three coins (sparkles, "+100", the score panel pops) |
| 1.4 s | lands on the next platform |
| 2.3 s | jumps on the enemy, which flashes red, squashes and dissolves |
| 2.95 s | grabs the jetpack: flies with the jets on, a ring of stars in orbit and a glow, and picks a row of coins |
| 6.5 to 8 s | lands on a wooden platform, hops back to the first one and the loop closes |

What it uses: the world and the parallax layers are bones moving at speeds that close the loop
(400, 320 and 160 px/s); coins spin and the enemy flaps by swapping images every frame; the
screen is a clipping mask, so nothing outside the 1280 x 720 view is drawn; the hero's jets,
orbit and glow are the same effects and particles as the showcase, tuned for a light sky (the
jet halo uses normal blend with a warm color instead of additive light).

Generated by `npx tsx exemplos/jogo/gerar.ts <folder>`, where the folder has the Kenney packs
extracted as `jumper-pack/extraido`, `ui-pack/extraido` and `particle-pack/extraido`
([kenney.nl/assets](https://kenney.nl/assets)). Art: Jumper Pack, UI Pack (and its Kenney
Future font) and Particle Pack by Kenney, CC0.

The GIF above is the editor's export of the 1280 x 720 screen (`"rect": [0, 0, 1280, 720]`) with a
footer and a credit, at 20 fps and 800 px wide.

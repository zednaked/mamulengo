# The photographer: depth of field with blur

![through the camera's viewfinder, the focus racks between the group and a monster in front of the lens, then the photo becomes a Polaroid](foco.gif)

`foco.boneco` is a story where the focus is the action, with the monsters of `exemplos/monstros`:
Clique (blue, the photographer), Três-Olhos (yellow), Ciclope (red), Gargalhada (green) and
Bagunça (brown, the one with four arms). We look through the viewfinder of Clique's camera (he stays out of frame) in one looping animation (`foto`, 10.5 s):

| time | what happens |
|---|---|
| 0.0 to 1.6 s | the viewfinder opens all blurred; the lens turns, the focus marker slides from "perto" (near) to the group, overshoots a little and settles (the AF hunting), and the AF brackets turn green |
| 1.6 to 2.9 s | the group waves; the brown one walks off to the right |
| 2.8 to 3.9 s | the brown one peeks in from the right edge, huge and out of focus, with a sly grin, then jumps to the middle with his mouth open ("BU!"); the focus jumps to him (the group blurs behind) and he pulls faces |
| 4.6 to 5.8 s | the photographer complains ("Ei! Sai da frente!"), the viewfinder shakes, the brown one leaves and the focus goes back to the group |
| 5.8 to 6.4 s | everyone poses, the brown one too, until he hops crooked with his tongue out right as the shutter blinks and the flash fires |
| 6.4 to 8.5 s | the photo freezes and drops as a Polaroid on a table, pale and grey at first, developing its colors: there he is, in mid-air |
| 8.5 to 10.5 s | a red pen draws an arrow and writes "Bagunça!!" (his name) on the photo while the camera moves in on him |

The blur is the core's adjustment layers (docs/NUCLEO.md "Ajuste"): one right above the park, one
right above the group (it blurs the group and, stacked, the park), and one masked by a soft
ellipse on top of the brown one up close. Each plane has a depth (park 1, group 0.72, the brown
one 0.17) and the lens has a focus distance that moves, overshoots and settles; each blur is
20 px per unit between its plane and the focus, so with the group in focus the park is soft too.
The viewfinder's focus marker shows the same focus distance. The Polaroid develops through one
more adjustment layer under its frame (saturation, lightness and contrast). The monsters are two instances of `exemplos/monstros` (animations
`foto_grupo` and `foto_careta`), and the group is frozen at the flash (instance speed 0).

Generated by `npx tsx exemplos/foco/gerar.ts` (after `exemplos/monstros/gerar.ts`). The park, the
viewfinder, the speech bubble, the mask and the table are drawn by the generator. The GIF is the
editor's export of `"rect": [-640, -360, 1280, 720]`.

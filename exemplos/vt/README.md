# "A foto da turma": a 30-second spot

![the group squeezed into a selfie, Clique joining in and Bagunça holding the stolen camera up](vt-quadro.png)

`vt.boneco` is a finished 26-second spot (about 30 s with Mamulengo's short opening and closing,
`tools/vinheta.ts`) with the monsters of `exemplos/monstros`: Clique (blue), Três-Olhos (yellow),
Ciclope (red), Gargalhada (green) and Bagunça (brown). Animation `vt`, in four shots:

| time | shot | what happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0 to 5.5 s | the park, wide | the group arrives one by one (Três-Olhos hopping, Ciclope dropping from the sky with a dust burst and a camera shake, Gargalhada sliding in, Bagunça rolling in and bumping into him); Clique, in the foreground, calls "Foto da turma!", raises his camera and the picture dives into the lens |
| 5.5 to 16.0 s | through the viewfinder | the whole photographer scene (`exemplos/foco`): rack focus, Bagunça photobombing, the scolding, the flash and the Polaroid with "Bagunça!!" |
| 16.0 to 20.75 s | the park, wide | Clique shows the photo, the group leans in and reacts (Gargalhada laughs, Ciclope facepalms, Bagunça is proud), Bagunça leaps behind Clique and steals the camera ("Ei! Minha câmera!"), squeezes everyone into a selfie, "Xiiis!", flash |
| 20.75 to 26.0 s | the table | the selfie drops onto the first photo, develops (pale and grey to color), a red pen writes "de novo!!" with an arrow at Bagunça, and the camera moves in |

How it is built: the shots are slots whose transparency switches on and off; the wide shots are
two instances of `exemplos/monstros` (animations `vt_grupo` and `vt_clique`, with the camera and
the photo as props in the monsters' hands), the viewfinder is an instance of `exemplos/foco`, and
the table shows that scene frozen at its end (instance speed 0) under the selfie (animation
`vt_selfie`). The park is slightly blurred behind the monsters (an adjustment layer), the selfie
develops through an adjustment layer masked by the photo window, the dust comes from the effects
library, and a lens vignette darkens the edges of the camera shots.

Generated by `npx tsx exemplos/vt/gerar.ts` (after `exemplos/monstros` and `exemplos/foco`). The
video is the editor's export of `"rect": [-640, -360, 1280, 720]` at 30 fps, with
`npx tsx tools/vinheta.ts vt.webm` for the opening and closing.
